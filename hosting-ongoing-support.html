CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Maintenance and new development aren't the same thing.

Included ongoing work

Routine maintenance and changes to the existing website or existing Elero-built system are covered according to your chosen support package.

New functionality

New pages, major redesigns, substantial new sections, new software modules or entirely new systems are quoted separately before work begins.

Third-party services

Charges from payment processors, messaging providers, specialist APIs and other external services remain separate unless explicitly included.

SEO

Initial SEO foundations are included. Managed websites receive ongoing technical SEO upkeep, while active search-growth campaigns are a separate service.

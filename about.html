About Elero Studio

Web design should feel straightforward.

We’re a small South Wales web studio building modern websites and useful digital solutions around the businesses that actually use them.

BASED IN South Wales
FOCUS Business websites
APPROACH Direct & personal
ED Founder, Elero Studio
01 / FOUNDER

Hi, I’m Ed — Founder of Elero Studio.

I founded Elero Studio with a clear idea of what I wanted the company to be: a modern web studio that gives businesses a more straightforward, personal and effective alternative to the traditional agency experience.

From the beginning, the vision has been about more than just building good-looking websites. Elero is designed to help businesses improve how they present themselves, how customers interact with them and how their digital systems work behind the scenes.

I set the direction for Elero, oversee the work we produce and make sure everything we build reflects the standard and approach the company was created around.

We’re deliberately a small studio. That means clients can speak directly with the people working on their website, get clear answers and know who they’re dealing with from the first conversation through to launch.

02 / HOW WE WORK

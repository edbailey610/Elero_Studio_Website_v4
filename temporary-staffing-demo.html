TEMPORARY STAFFING PORTAL DEMO

Let your candidates log in and choose their own shifts.

This is an example of a temporary staffing portal Elero could build into a recruitment company's website. Candidates can log in, see their working week, view shifts available on each day, accept the work they want and tell the recruitment agency when they're available.

CANDIDATE CALENDAR See the whole working week.

Candidates see available shifts directly on a weekly calendar instead of scrolling through job listings.

CHOOSE WORK Accept a shift online.

Pay, hours, location and employer are visible before the candidate decides whether to take the shift.

RECRUITER DASHBOARD See who's covering what.

Recruiters can see staffing levels across the same working week from their own dashboard.

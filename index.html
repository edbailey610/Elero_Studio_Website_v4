03 / PRICING

Clear pricing. No surprise charges.

Your website has a one-off build price agreed before we start. There is no deposit and nothing to pay upfront. After launch, you simply choose the ongoing option that suits you.

STEP 1 · WEBSITE BUILD

Your agreed website quote

The exact build price depends on the size and functionality of your website. You receive a clear quote before any work begins.

£0 deposit → We build it → You approve it → You pay the quote
STEP 2 · AFTER LAUNCH

Choose one monthly option.

You do not pay both. Your first 30 days of reasonable post-launch edits are included whichever option you choose.

ESSENTIALOPTION 01
£3.99/ month

Website Hosting

For clients who simply want us to keep their website hosted, online and accessible.

  • Website hosting
  • Your website stays live
  • 30 days of post-launch edits included
  • Ongoing edits are not included after the 30-day window
MANAGEDOPTION 02
£39.99/ month

Managed Website

For clients who want hosting plus ongoing help making reasonable changes to their live website.

  • Website hosting included
  • Ongoing reasonable amendments
  • 30 days of post-launch edits included
  • No need to pay £3.99 as well — this replaces the hosting-only option
GOOD TO KNOW

Larger redesigns, new pages or substantial new functionality fall outside normal amendments and would always be quoted separately before work is carried out.
